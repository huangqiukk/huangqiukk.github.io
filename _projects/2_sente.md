**Program:** 千手・文殊プロジェクト（フェーズ1）若手研究者支援プログラム  
**Period:** 2026–2027  
**Institution:** Nara Institute of Science and Technology (NAIST)

This program supports early-career researchers at NAIST to develop independent research initiatives. The project focuses on advancing research in enzyme-embedded biodegradable polymer systems and enzyme stabilization under anhydrous, high-temperature conditions.
